\section*{Scope and configuration}
This guide describes the new in-memory preparation services, not a completed
FITS-to-ASDF recipe or an accepted physical calibration. Actual Mac qualification
and real corrected-ROI products remain separate gates. The reviewed design is
in the developer design directory:\par
\texttt{linearity-preparation-overscan-asdf-v1.md}.

The detector TOML describes the full detector bands and dimensions. The
processing TOML selects the sample columns, trim rectangle, rejection counts,
ROIs and preparation controls. The shared Python resolver validates their
relationship and freezes both original texts and SHA256 identities. There is no
PAF fallback. For CCD65, the full overscan is columns 2097--2144, but the sample
is 2100--2142. These distinctions survive in the resolved record.

\section*{Exact row correction and arithmetic}
For raw frame $f$, raw row $y$, and the $n$ enabled scan values sorted in
ascending order, the existing trimmed-mean routine returns
\begin{equation}
O_f(y)=\operatorname{pix}\!\left[
 \frac{\sum_{k=l+1}^{n-h}v_{f,y,(k)}}{n-l-h}\right].
\end{equation}
The sum and division use double precision; $\operatorname{pix}$ converts to
the native \texttt{pixelvalue} type. Low/high rejection is $l=h=10$ in the
selected OmegaCAM profile: 43 reads retain 23, and 44 retain 24. No sigma
clipping, smoothing, row fitting, endpoint extrapolation or global-level
replacement is introduced. First and last raw rows use their own scan samples.
The active-pixel BPM is not applied to electronic scan reads.

The native stages are
\begin{align}
U_f(x,y)&=\operatorname{pix}[D_f(x,y)-O_f(y)],\\
S_f(p)&=\operatorname{pix}[U_f(p)-B(p)]\quad\text{(flats only)}.
\end{align}
The trim uses inclusive FITS coordinates after scan subtraction. An active
row $j$ maps to raw row $j+y_{\min}-1$. Preserve both native roundings: do not
merge the subtractions. A supplied master is already scan-corrected and active;
it is subtracted once and receives no second scan correction. Bias-role frame
preparation has no master subtraction. The existing bias service performs
frame admission and stacking; this increment does not change its median or
rejection policy. Master lineage compatibility must be verified by the future
file/recipe admission layer, not inferred from its pixel values.

\section*{Corrected ROI statistics and populations}
The paired service first reuses the unchanged raw-pair measurement service.
For each configured ROI it uses the common population $\mathcal P$ admitted
by the declared BPM and finite/raw-saturation checks in both flats and both
raw biases. The raw saturation boundary remains 65534 ADU. Pair members retain
their original identity, HDU, input index and exact duration.

For corrected member values $S_{1p},S_{2p}$, let $N=|\mathcal P|$. The services
retain ordinary means and sample scatter of each member and their difference:
\begin{align}
\overline S_i&=N^{-1}\sum_{p\in\mathcal P}S_{ip},\\
s_i^2&=(N-1)^{-1}\sum_{p\in\mathcal P}(S_{ip}-\overline S_i)^2,\\
d_p&=\operatorname{pix}[S_{1p}-S_{2p}],\qquad
s_d^2=(N-1)^{-1}\sum_{p\in\mathcal P}(d_p-\overline d)^2.
\end{align}
Difference pixel arithmetic follows \texttt{image\_pair\_stats\_checked}.
The native result stores standard deviations and availability; a later
serialization stage may store their squared values and $s_d^2/2$ with explicit
definitions. For $N<2$, sample scatter is unavailable, not zero. A corrected
nonfinite value cannot silently remove an otherwise admitted measured pixel:
all three corrected population counts must equal the raw count.

Spatial scatter and difference scatter are diagnostic quantities, not assigned
independent weights. The shared master cancels algebraically in differences,
subject to native rounding. Raw bias-only RON and raw gain diagnostics retain
their original definitions. No uncertainty model or physical response is
estimated. ROI means remain distinct from the direct physical pixel samples
required by the later nonlinear model.

\section*{Connection to implementation and verification}
\texttt{pycavi\_linearity\_config.resolve} performs TOML resolution only.

\texttt{mef\_linearity\_prepare\_frame} validates geometry and finite enabled
scan reads, copies the borrowed raw image and uses the existing
\texttt{mef\_overscan\_correction\_traced} arithmetic. Its optional trace copies
each estimate before QC median sorting, preserving physical row order.
The legacy interfaces pass a null trace and keep their numerical behavior.
The result owns the active image, trace and settings copy.

\texttt{mef\_linearity\_prepare\_pairs} corrects the two biases for trace evidence
and two flats at a time, reusing raw-pair admission and the existing masked
single-image and difference statistics. Results own copied identities, raw
diagnostics, corrected ROI statistics and traces. Borrowed inputs stay unchanged;
occupied outputs, invalid settings, resource limits and failures do not expose
partial completed owners. Custom allocation checks cover the new owners and
snapshot owners; they do not establish general Eclipse allocation safety.

Independent ramp fixtures cover both scan widths, raw/active edges, exact
singleton-path agreement, common-population analytical moments, and persistent
owner-allocation failures. Existing bias-service, sample and raw-pair controls
check affected shared paths. Six TOML and five footprint controls check the
configuration boundary. Agent Linux success is not actual Mac acceptance or
scientific validation. Complete recipe/ASDF orchestration follows that gate.
